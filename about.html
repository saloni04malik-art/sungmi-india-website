WHO WE ARE

BUILT FOR DEMANDING MARINE ENVIRONMENTS

Sungmi India Private Limited was established in December 2018 as a joint venture between India Futuristic Marine Pvt. Ltd. and Sungmi Co., Ltd., South Korea.

The company specializes in the manufacturing and supply of accommodation systems for ships, oil and gas, rigs, platforms, wind motors, and offshore HVAC sub-stations, including fire-resistant doors, wall panels, ceiling panels & wet units/toilet modules.
