WHO WE ARE

BUILT FOR DEMANDING MARINE ENVIRONMENTS

Sungmi India Private Limited was established in December 2018 as a joint venture between India Futuristic Marine Pvt. Ltd. and Sungmi Co., Ltd., South Korea.

The company specializes in the manufacturing and supply of accommodation systems for ships, oil and gas, rigs, platforms, wind motors, and offshore HVAC sub-stations, including fire-resistant doors, wall panels, ceiling panels & wet units/toilet modules.

INSIGHTS & KNOWLEDGE

TECHNICAL INSIGHTS & EXPERT KNOWLEDGE

Explore key engineering insights, fire safety standards, and modern modular accommodation practices for marine and offshore applications.

MARINE ENGINEERING

Why Marine Accommodation Systems Matter in Modern Shipbuilding

Marine accommodation is a critical structural subsystem directly influencing crew endurance, acoustic insulation, vessel balance, and statutory classification compliance.

MARINE SAFETY

The Role of Fire-Resistant Doors in Marine Accommodation

In maritime safety engineering, fire-resistant doors act as vital thermal barriers designed to compartmentalize fire, prevent smoke migration, and preserve emergency evacuation corridors.

OFFSHORE ENGINEERING

Building Accommodation Systems for Offshore Environments

Offshore oil rigs, FPSOs, platforms, and wind substations face extreme environmental loads, saline corrosion, high humidity, and continuous structural vibrations.

MODULAR SYSTEMS

Modular Accommodation: Smarter Solutions for Marine Projects

Factory prefabrication of modular cabins and wet units streamlines shipyard schedules, reduces on-board hot work, and ensures consistent quality control under controlled industrial standards.
